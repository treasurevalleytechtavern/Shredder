About the Show

Fast riffs, shouted choruses, and the kind of energy that makes hardcore shows unforgettable.

Boston hardcore band Haywire takes over The Shredder in Boise on Wednesday, March 17, 2027, joined by Philadelphia punks Dark Thoughts and genre-bending artist GVLLOW.

Known for their explosive live performances and relentless touring schedule, Haywire 617 blends old-school hardcore aggression with street punk attitude and huge sing-along choruses. Their 2026 album, *I Don't Wanna Say Goodbye*, builds on that sound with more melody, plenty of grit, and songs about friendship, heartbreak, and life on the road.

Dark Thoughts brings fast, stripped-down punk rock straight out of Philadelphia, with Ramones-inspired hooks, raw guitars, and songs that waste absolutely no time getting to the point.

Rounding out the lineup, GVLLOW mixes punk rock, post-punk, darkwave, and hip-hop influences into a sound that's dark, melodic, and distinctly his own.

Three artists with different approaches to punk and alternative music, all sharing one stage. From breakneck hardcore and infectious punk hooks to moody, atmospheric sounds, this is a night with plenty to sink your teeth into.

Presented by Duck Club Presents.
