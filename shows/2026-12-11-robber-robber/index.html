About the Show

Some bands play it safe. Robber Robber isn't one of them.

The Burlington, Vermont quartet brings its jagged blend of post-punk, noise rock, and experimental indie rock to The Shredder in Boise on Friday, December 11, 2026, alongside Brooklyn's Bloodsports.

Led by vocalist and guitarist Nina Cates, Robber Robber combines distorted guitars, off-kilter rhythms, and deceptively catchy melodies into songs that rarely go where you expect. Their 2026 album, *Two Wheels Move the Soul*, turns personal upheaval into restless, explosive guitar rock, shifting between driving grooves, bursts of noise, and moments of unexpected beauty.

Joining them, Bloodsports brings a darker mix of post-hardcore, slowcore, and noise rock. Their debut album, *Anything Can Be A Hammer*, balances brooding melodies with crushing distortion and raw emotional intensity.

For fans of adventurous indie rock, gritty post-punk, and bands that aren't afraid to get weird, this lineup has plenty to dig into.

Presented by Duck Club Presents.
