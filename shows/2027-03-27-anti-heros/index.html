About the Show

No frills, no polish, just loud, unapologetic street punk.

Anti-Heros bring decades of American Oi! and punk rock history to The Shredder in Boise on Saturday, March 27, 2027, joined by Blue Collar Criminals, Die Fighting, and Steelbound.

Formed in Atlanta, Georgia, in 1984, Anti-Heros helped shape the American street punk scene with hard-hitting riffs, rough-edged vocals, and anthemic choruses made for shouting back at the stage. From early records like *That's Right!* and *Don't Tread on Me* to their more recent material, the band has built a legacy on raw, uncompromising punk rock.

Arizona's Blue Collar Criminals keep that working-class spirit alive with their own blend of Oi!, hardcore, and street rock 'n' roll. Die Fighting and Steelbound round out a four-band bill rooted in the underground punk scene.

For fans of classic American Oi!, no-nonsense hardcore, and the kind of punk show where the crowd is part of the music, this one's worth getting loud for.
