About the Show

Boise in February isn't exactly beach weather. Surf Hat doesn't seem too concerned.

Hailing from Squamish, British Columbia, Surf Hat brings its breezy blend of psychedelic surf rock and indie to The Shredder in Boise on Tuesday, February 2, 2027.

What started as a spontaneous idea between friends on a beach has grown into a band known for catchy melodies, reverb-soaked guitars, and effortlessly groovy rhythms. Drawing inspiration from The Growlers, Allah-Las, and Talking Heads, Surf Hat puts its own spin on surf rock with a sound that's equal parts nostalgic, playful, and refreshingly unpredictable.

Their 2025 album, *Modern World Blues*, explores new territory while keeping the easygoing energy that makes their music so enjoyable. With three full-length albums and plenty of touring under their belts, Surf Hat has built a reputation for turning laid-back songs into lively performances.

For fans of psychedelic rock, surf-inspired guitar music, and indie bands that know how to find a groove, this is a good excuse to get out on a Tuesday night.

Presented by Duck Club Presents.
