About the Show

Turning 45 deserves a little noise.

JSIN's 45th Birthday Bash brings a night of Boise punk rock to The Shredder on Saturday, December 5, 2026, featuring So Down!, Far From Giants, Chris Raney, and DJ JSIN.

Boise's So Down! delivers fast, melodic punk rock packed with driving guitars, big hooks, and plenty of heart. Drawing inspiration from bands like Face to Face, Rise Against, and A Wilhelm Scream, their sound balances raw energy with lyrics that actually mean something.

Joining them are fellow Boise punk rockers Far From Giants, bringing their own hard-hitting sound to the celebration, alongside Chris Raney and DJ JSIN.

A birthday bash built around local music, loud guitars, and the people who keep Boise's punk scene alive. Sounds like a pretty good way to celebrate 45.
