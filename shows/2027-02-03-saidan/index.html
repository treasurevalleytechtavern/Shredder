About the Show

Black metal doesn't have to follow the rules, and Saidan and Trhä are proof of that.

Two distinct approaches to underground black metal meet at The Shredder in Boise on Wednesday, February 3, 2027, joined by Barbelith for a night of blistering guitars, haunting atmosphere, and music that pushes well beyond the genre's traditional boundaries.

Hailing from Nashville, Tennessee, Saidan combines raw, melodic black metal with Japanese horror influences and the theatrical intensity of visual kei. Their music moves between relentless blast beats, razor-sharp riffs, and dark storytelling, including their 2026 album, *Fangdriller: Scars Beneath Memory's Wrist*.

Trhä, the atmospheric black metal project of multi-instrumentalist Damián Ojeda, takes a different approach. Layers of distorted guitars, distant vocals, and immersive soundscapes create an otherworldly experience, complete with a fictional language and mythology of its own.

Baltimore's Barbelith adds another dimension with atmospheric and post-black metal that balances raw intensity with sweeping, emotional passages.

For fans of atmospheric black metal, experimental extreme music, and bands willing to take the genre somewhere unexpected, this is a lineup worth exploring.
