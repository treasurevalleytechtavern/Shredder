About the Show

If Brokencyde and Dot Dot Curve were on your MySpace playlist, you already know what kind of chaos you're in for.

A Very Scary Krunkmas brings the unmistakable sound of late-2000s crunkcore to The Shredder in Boise, Idaho, on November 25, 2026. Expect a collision of screamo vocals, hip-hop beats, electronic sounds, and the kind of outrageous energy that made this scene impossible to ignore.

Brokencyde and Dot Dot Curve lead the lineup alongside Auntie Boy, with A War In Me, ReaperDa Skid, Laurance, and IZ & The Friendship Gang rounding out the night.

For longtime fans, it's a chance to hear the music that defined an entire corner of internet culture at full volume. For anyone discovering crunkcore for the first time, there's no better introduction than experiencing it live.

Presented by Dyna Duo and Grimm Entertainment.
